Character Profile

Details and personality

熵尾音 こうせい (Kousei), also written 熵尾音 咬青

Wolf beastman · Male · Approximately 17 · Birthday: August 14 · About 168 cm

Kousei comes from outer space and is one of the independent offshoots of an unknown entity. He dozes off easily, and his actions are often guided by his subconscious. He was born with his facial markings and twin tails, both of which can change depending on the state he’s in. He’s especially fond of watermelon.

Voice A powerful voice with a confident delivery.
Features Facial markings and twin tails that change with his state
Likes Watermelon
Twin tails The twin tails Kousei was born with give him a natural advantage when it comes to balance. They’re soft and fluffy and hold in warmth exceptionally well. In his sleep, he often instinctively curls both tails around himself.
Collar microphone The collar uses a directional array of over a hundred million nanosensors to pick up the movements Kousei makes as he produces sound, along with the vibrations of his vocal folds. It builds an acoustic model of him in real time. From those physical measurements alone, it can create and record an accurate digital reconstruction of any sound he makes.
