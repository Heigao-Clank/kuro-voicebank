## Usage Instructions
Recommended engine: HiFiUTAU works best with this voicebank.
Engine GitHub: https://github.com/xiaobaijunya/HiFiUTAU-engine

The three voice styles are Original (modal voice, _O), Falsetto (_F) and Soft (_S).
  Main: Uses Original up to and including A#4, and Falsetto from B4 upwards.
  Original: Modal voice.
  Falsetto: Falsetto voice.
  Soft: A gentle voice, as if murmuring to oneself.

## Character Profile

【Basic Information】
Name: 熵尾音 こうせい (Kousei), also written 熵尾音 咬青
Age: Approximately 17
Birthday: August 14
Gender: Male
Height: About 168 cm
Species: Wolf beastman

【Character Description】
Kousei comes from outer space and is one of the independent offshoots of an unknown entity. He dozes off easily, and his actions are often guided by his subconscious. He was born with his facial markings and twin tails, both of which can change depending on the state he’s in. He’s especially fond of watermelon.

Twin tails:
The twin tails Kousei was born with give him a natural advantage when it comes to balance. They’re soft and fluffy and hold in warmth exceptionally well. In his sleep, he often instinctively curls both tails around himself.

Collar microphone:
The collar uses a directional array of over a hundred million nanosensors to pick up the movements Kousei makes as he produces sound, along with the vibrations of his vocal folds. It builds an acoustic model of him in real time. From those physical measurements alone, it can create and record an accurate digital reconstruction of any sound he makes.
